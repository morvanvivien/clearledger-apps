Support

Write to hola@tutiendapropiaweb.com with the app name, your site URL and what you see (a screenshot helps). We answer in English or Spanish, usually within two business days.

Before you write

  • Check that you are a Jira or Confluence administrator: admin features are restricted on purpose.
  • For GroupScope, read the coverage notes at the top of the app: some places (global permissions, private filters) cannot be read through Atlassian's APIs.
  • For SortKeeper, "Undo last sort" restores the previous order of the last manual sort.

Security

Report a security issue to the same address with "SECURITY" in the subject. We acknowledge within two business days.
