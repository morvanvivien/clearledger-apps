Privacy Policy: LinkLens for Confluence

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Publisher: Vivien Morvan, trading as ClearLedger Apps, Mendoza, Argentina

Contact: hola@tutiendapropiaweb.com

This policy explains what LinkLens for Confluence ("the app") does with data. It is written to be short and exact.

1. In one paragraph

LinkLens is an Atlassian Forge app. It runs and stores data on the Atlassian platform, inside the Atlassian cloud site where an administrator installed it. The publisher does not receive, copy or access your data, and the app sends nothing to any other service.

2. Where the app runs

  • All code runs on Atlassian Forge infrastructure.
  • All data is stored in Forge storage attached to your Atlassian site, in the data residency region applicable to your site.
  • The app declares no external network access (no "egress"). It cannot send data to the publisher or to any third party, and it uses no analytics, tracking, advertising or error-reporting service.

3. What data the app reads

Through the Confluence REST API, with read-only permissions:

  • The body of current pages (storage format), only to find which pages they link to. The text is processed in memory and not stored.
  • Page identifiers, titles and space keys, to match links.
  • When someone opens the macro or the orphan report: titles, space names and the last-modified date of the linking pages, and the display name of their last editor, read as the viewing user, so that only what that user may see is returned.

The app does not read comments, attachments, whiteboards, databases or user profiles beyond the display name of the last editor.

4. What data the app stores

In Forge storage on your site:

  • The link index: pairs of page identifiers (page A links to page B) and, for links written by title, a one-way hash of the space key and title. No titles, text, names or e-mail addresses.
  • The state of the indexing (status, counters, dates, short error messages). Readers of the macro are only told a state (building, ready or failed); counters and errors are visible only in the app logs of the site's developer console.

Data is deleted when the app is uninstalled, according to Atlassian's Forge storage rules.

5. What the app shows

Titles, spaces, editor names and dates of linking pages are shown only to a user who can already view those pages in Confluence. The app does not reveal the number or the existence of pages the user cannot view.

6. Sharing and subprocessors

The publisher shares no data with anyone because it receives none. Atlassian is the platform provider and processes data under its own terms.

7. Retention

The index follows the content: it is refreshed from page events and a periodic check. Index entries of pages that were deleted, trashed or archived are removed by the end of the next full pass, which happens at least every 8 days (and immediately for archived pages). Entries never outlive the pages for more than that. Removal of the app removes the stored index.

8. Your rights

Because the publisher holds no personal data from your site, requests about personal data in Confluence go to your site administrator or to Atlassian. For anything about this app, write to hola@tutiendapropiaweb.com.

9. Changes

Material changes will be announced in the Marketplace listing and release notes.
