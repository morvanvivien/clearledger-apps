Privacy Policy: GroupScope for Jira

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Publisher: Vivien Morvan, trading as ClearLedger Apps, Mendoza, Argentina

Contact: hola@tutiendapropiaweb.com

This policy explains what GroupScope for Jira ("the app") does with data. It is written to be short and exact.

1. In one paragraph

GroupScope is an Atlassian Forge app. It runs and stores data on the Atlassian platform, inside the Atlassian cloud site where an administrator installed it. The publisher does not receive, copy or access your data, and the app sends nothing to any other service.

2. Where the app runs

  • All code runs on Atlassian Forge infrastructure.
  • All data is stored in Forge storage attached to your Atlassian site, in the data residency region applicable to your site.
  • The app declares no external network access (no "egress"). It cannot send data to the publisher or to any third party, and it uses no third-party analytics, tracking, advertising or error-reporting service.

3. What data the app reads

When an administrator runs a scan, the app reads the following from your Jira site through the Jira REST API, with read-only permissions:

  • Group names and identifiers, and the members of each group: Atlassian account identifier and active/inactive status. Jira returns some profile fields together with each member (for example the display name); the app discards them and keeps only the account identifier and the active/inactive status. E-mail addresses are not requested.
  • Permission schemes and the groups they grant permissions to, and which projects use each scheme.
  • Project roles and the groups assigned to them in each project.
  • Issue security schemes and levels, and notification schemes, where a group is referenced.
  • Names, owner account identifier and sharing settings of filters and dashboards shared with a group.
  • The site name and URL, to label exports.

The app does not read issues, comments, attachments, issue content, worklogs or any customer content.

Display names are read live, and only when needed. When an administrator opens the members of a group or the owner of a filter or dashboard, compares two snapshots, or exports members, usages or a comparison as CSV, the app asks Jira (read-only call "Bulk get users", up to 50 account identifiers per request) for the display name of the accounts concerned. The names are shown on screen or written in the file the administrator downloads. The app does not save them, and does not keep them between two requests.

4. What data the app stores

In Forge storage on your site, compressed:

  • The result of the latest scan.
  • Snapshots taken manually or automatically, with their date and type.
  • One setting: the frequency of automatic snapshots.
  • Temporary working data during a scan, deleted when the scan ends.

The only personal data in the scan result and in the snapshots are Atlassian account identifiers (the members of each group, and the owner of each filter or dashboard shared with a group) and the active/inactive status of members. The app stores no display name, no e-mail address and no other profile information of any person.

The app does not store data about who clicked what. Technical logs written by the app on Forge contain no personal data (only error types and counts); Atlassian operates the Forge logging system.

5. What the publisher can see

Nothing from your site. The publisher only receives what Atlassian provides to all Marketplace partners about licences and sales (for example the site, the licence tier and billing details), under Atlassian's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Exports

CSV files are generated on request and downloaded by your browser. The app does not keep a copy outside the snapshots described above. Display names in exports are looked up in Jira at the time of the export and exist only in the downloaded file, next to the account identifiers. What you do with an exported file is under your responsibility; exports of members, filter and dashboard owners and snapshot comparisons contain names of users.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Snapshots and the latest scan stay until an administrator deletes them (snapshots can be deleted in the app) or the app is uninstalled. At most 50 snapshots are kept.
  • When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian retains Forge app data for a limited period (28 days according to Atlassian's documentation) and then deletes it. Atlassian's policies apply to that stage.

8. Security

The app uses the Atlassian Forge security model: data isolated per installation, encryption of stored data and of data in transit managed by Atlassian, authentication through Forge OAuth. The app requests only read scopes and Forge storage. It never changes Jira configuration. The user interface displays Jira-provided names as plain text only.

9. Sub-processors

The publisher uses no sub-processor for this app. Atlassian acts as the hosting platform, under Atlassian's terms.

10. Your rights

Because the publisher holds no copy of your data, requests to access, correct or delete personal data in Jira or in the app's storage are handled by your site's administrators. They can delete snapshots in the app or uninstall it. The app's storage holds account identifiers and an active/inactive status only, no name and no e-mail address, so a change to a person's name in Atlassian applies the next time that name is displayed. For questions about this policy, write to hola@tutiendapropiaweb.com.

11. Changes

If the app starts to use a new category of data or an external service, this policy will be updated before release, and the Marketplace listing and Atlassian's Forge review will show the change (new scopes or egress require administrator approval).
